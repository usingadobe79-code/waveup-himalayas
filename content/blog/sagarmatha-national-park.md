Sagarmatha National Park, located in the Khumbu region of northeastern Nepal, is a spectacular Himalayan destination famous for Mount Everest, breathtaking mountain landscapes, glaciers, and diverse wildlife. Established in 1976 and recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1979, **Sagarmatha National Park Nepal** attracts trekkers, nature lovers, and adventure seekers from around the world. The park offers access to iconic destinations such as Everest Base Camp, Namche Bazaar, Tengboche Monastery, and the beautiful Gokyo Lakes. Visitors can explore scenic **Sagarmatha National Park trekking routes**, discover traditional Sherpa culture, and learn about the region’s unique biodiversity. ## How to Reach Sagarmatha National Park from Kathmandu

Reaching Sagarmatha National Park from Kathmandu involves travelling by air or road before continuing on foot through the beautiful Khumbu region of Nepal. Since roads do not connect directly to the park's main trekking destinations, most travellers fly to Lukla and begin trekking from there. The journey offers spectacular Himalayan scenery, traditional Sherpa villages, suspension bridges, and breathtaking mountain valleys.

### By Flight from Kathmandu to Lukla

![](/images/blog/media-b51be4d2-1767086591-001.jpg "Lukla Airport")

Flying from Kathmandu to Lukla is the most popular option for travellers planning to visit Sagarmatha National Park and trek to Everest Base Camp. The flight generally takes around 30–35 minutes, depending on operating conditions, and offers spectacular views of Nepal's hills and Himalayan mountains.

During peak trekking seasons, particularly spring and autumn, some flights operate from Manthali Airport in Ramechhap rather than Kathmandu. Travellers may need to arrange an additional road journey to reach the departure airport. Flights to Lukla are highly weather-dependent, so delays and cancellations are possible.

After arriving in Lukla, trekkers typically walk towards Phakding and continue to Namche Bazaar, an important Sherpa settlement and acclimatization stop on the Everest Base Camp route.

### By Road and Trekking via Salleri or Phaplu

![](/images/blog/1594544603106453373_760778134661645_2946194967409683425_n%20%281%29-001.jpg "Salleri to Sagarmatha National Park")

Travellers who prefer an overland journey can take a jeep from Kathmandu to Salleri or Phaplu in the Solukhumbu region. The journey can take much of a day or longer, depending on road conditions, traffic, and weather.

From Salleri or Phaplu, visitors continue on foot along mountain trails through traditional villages, forests, suspension bridges, and scenic valleys. Depending on the starting point and itinerary, reaching the main Everest trekking trail can require several additional days of walking.

This option is suitable for travellers who have more time and want to experience rural Nepal beyond the main Everest trekking route. However, rough roads, landslides, and seasonal weather can affect travel times, so flexibility is important.

### By the Classic Trekking Route via Jiri

![](/images/blog/bhandara-jiri-ebc-trek%20%281%29.jpg "Jiri to Sagarmatha National Park")

Another option is to travel by road from Kathmandu to Jiri and continue trekking towards the Everest region. This historic approach was used by early Everest expeditions before flights to Lukla became the main access route.

The trail passes through green hills, rural settlements, forests, and mountain landscapes before connecting with routes towards the Khumbu region. Although this journey requires considerably more time than flying to Lukla, it offers a deeper experience of Nepal's countryside and traditional village life.

Travellers choosing this route should allow several additional trekking days and prepare for long walking stages, changing weather, and basic accommodation in some settlements.

### By Helicopter from Kathmandu

![](/images/blog/Prabhuhelicopter_20230120133300-001.jpg)

Helicopter travel is another option for visitors interested in reaching the Everest region with less trekking time or experiencing panoramic views of the Himalayas.

Depending on weather, permissions, and operating arrangements, helicopter services may travel to destinations in the Lukla and Everest region. This option is generally more expensive than regular flights and overland travel.

Travellers should consider altitude safety carefully, as flying directly to high elevations without adequate acclimatization can increase the risk of altitude sickness. Always confirm the itinerary, landing arrangements, and applicable restrictions with the operator.

### Trekking from Lukla to Sagarmatha National Park

After arriving in Lukla, visitors continue on foot through the Khumbu Valley. The following table outlines approximate walking times for common sections of the Everest Base Camp trekking route.

| Trekking route | Approximate walking time |
| --- | --- |
| Lukla to Phakding | 3–4 hours |
| Phakding to Namche Bazaar | 5–7 hours |
| Namche Bazaar to Tengboche | 5–6 hours |
| Tengboche to Dingboche | 5–6 hours |

Actual walking times vary depending on fitness, pace, weather, trail conditions, and rest breaks. The entrance to Sagarmatha National Park is near Monjo, along the trail between Phakding and Namche Bazaar. Visitors should follow current entry procedures and carry the required documentation.

## Top Attractions and Places to Visit in Sagarmatha National Park

Sagarmatha National Park is home to some of Nepal's most breathtaking Himalayan landscapes, traditional Sherpa villages, ancient monasteries, and world-famous trekking destinations. From the iconic Mount Everest to the turquoise Gokyo Lakes, the park offers unforgettable experiences for trekkers, nature lovers, and adventure seekers. At Wave Up Himalayas, we recommend exploring these remarkable places to discover the natural beauty and cultural heritage of the Everest region.

### Mount Everest

![](/images/blog/1596997438-001.opt.jpg "Mount Everest")

Mount Everest, known as Sagarmatha in Nepali and Chomolungma in Tibetan, is the world's highest mountain above sea level, reaching 8,848.86 metres. It is the most famous landmark in Sagarmatha National Park and a major attraction for travellers visiting Nepal. The surrounding Himalayan scenery, glaciers, and dramatic mountain ridges create exceptional opportunities for trekking and photography. Visitors can enjoy views of Everest from several viewpoints along established trekking routes without attempting a summit expedition.

### Everest Base Camp

![](/images/blog/Everest-Base-Camp-Header.jpg "Everest Base Camp")

[**Everest Base Camp** ](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/everest-base-camp-trek/)is one of the most popular trekking destinations in Nepal, located at approximately 5,364 metres above sea level on the southern side of Mount Everest. The trek passes through beautiful mountain villages, suspension bridges, pine forests, and rugged glacial landscapes. Along the way, trekkers experience the hospitality of Sherpa communities and spectacular views of surrounding Himalayan peaks. Reaching base camp requires proper preparation, a gradual acclimatization schedule, and several days of trekking.

### Namche Bazaar

![](/images/blog/9c2263b98f1e4736fb13c7aaaab8f474%20%281%29.jpg "Namche Bazzar")

Namche Bazaar is a vibrant Sherpa town and an important stop on the Everest Base Camp trekking route. Situated on a mountainside, it offers accommodation, restaurants, bakeries, shops, and stunning views of the surrounding mountains. Many trekkers spend an acclimatization day here before continuing to higher elevations. Visitors can explore local markets, learn about Sherpa culture, and enjoy nearby viewpoints while preparing for the next stage of their Himalayan adventure.

### Tengboche Monastery

![](/images/blog/Tengboche_Monastery.jpg "Tengboche Monastery")

Tengboche Monastery is one of the most significant Buddhist monasteries in the Everest region. Surrounded by spectacular Himalayan scenery, it provides visitors with an opportunity to learn about Tibetan Buddhist traditions and the spiritual heritage of Sherpa communities. The monastery is a memorable stop for trekkers travelling towards Everest Base Camp, and the surrounding area offers beautiful mountain views. Visitors should respect religious customs, follow local instructions, and ask permission before taking photographs inside religious spaces.

### Gokyo Lakes

![](/images/blog/Gokyo_Lakes__Cho_La_Pass__Everest_Base_Camp_Trek_desktop.jpg "Gokyo Lake")

The Gokyo Lakes are a series of stunning high-altitude lakes known for their turquoise and emerald-coloured waters. Surrounded by rugged Himalayan peaks and glaciers, they offer a quieter alternative to some of the busier Everest trekking routes. The Gokyo region is also popular for its scenic viewpoints and access to Gokyo Ri, which provides panoramic views of Everest and neighbouring mountains in suitable conditions. Travellers can explore the lakes through a dedicated[ **Gokyo trek**](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/everest-base-camp-gokyo-lake-trek/) or a longer itinerary combining Gokyo Lakes with Everest Base Camp.

### Kala Patthar

![](/images/blog/media-4e593b0a-1763444924-001.jpg "Kala Patthar")

Kala Patthar is a famous Himalayan viewpoint near Everest Base Camp, situated at approximately 5,545 metres above sea level. It is particularly popular for sunrise hikes and panoramic views of Mount Everest, Nuptse, and surrounding peaks. Although the climb is relatively short compared with the overall trek, the high elevation makes it physically demanding. Trekkers should attempt the ascent only when properly acclimatized and when weather and trail conditions are suitable.

### Khumbu Glacier

![](/images/blog/khumbu-icefall.jpg)

The Khumbu Glacier is one of the most remarkable natural features of Sagarmatha National Park. Located beneath the towering Himalayan peaks, it forms a dramatic landscape of ice, rock, and glacial debris. The glacier is an important feature of the Everest Base Camp region and highlights the powerful geological processes shaping the Himalayas. Visitors should remain on established trails and follow their guide's advice when travelling near glacial terrain.

### Khumjung Village

![](/images/blog/841c600b8524128d5383705f6aab86cef2a4164abb94edb841ee15d76f347844-001.jpg "Khumjung Village")

Khumjung is a traditional Sherpa village located above Namche Bazaar. Known for its stone houses, peaceful surroundings, and cultural heritage, it offers an opportunity to experience local life away from the main trekking trail. Visitors can explore the village, learn about Sherpa traditions, and appreciate views of the surrounding mountains. Khumjung is a worthwhile stop for travellers interested in the cultural side of Sagarmatha National Park.

### Dingboche Village

![](/images/blog/IMG_20201208_171843-1025.jpg "Dingboche Village")

Dingboche is an important high-altitude settlement along the Everest Base Camp trekking route. Surrounded by rugged mountain scenery, stone-walled fields, and open alpine landscapes, it is commonly used as an acclimatization stop before trekkers continue towards Lobuche and Everest Base Camp. The village provides basic accommodation and meals for trekkers and serves as a useful place to rest while adjusting to the increasing elevation.

### Ama Dablam

![](/images/blog/AR506852-scaled-001.jpg "Ama Dablam")

Ama Dablam is one of the most recognisable mountains in the Everest region, admired for its distinctive shape and steep ridges. Its name is often interpreted as referring to a mother's necklace, inspired by the mountain's appearance and hanging glacier. Trekkers can enjoy impressive views of Ama Dablam from several sections of the Khumbu trail, particularly around Tengboche and Dingboche. The mountain is also a renowned destination for experienced mountaineers.

## Wildlife and Biodiversity of Sagarmatha National Park

Sagarmatha National Park is not only famous for Mount Everest and its spectacular Himalayan landscapes but also for its unique wildlife and rich biodiversity. Located in the high-altitude Khumbu region of Nepal, the park contains diverse ecosystems, ranging from pine and birch forests to alpine meadows, rocky slopes, and glaciers. These habitats support a variety of animals and plants that have adapted to the cold temperatures, thin air, and challenging conditions of the Himalayas.

The wildlife of Sagarmatha National Park is an important part of Nepal’s natural heritage. The park also plays a vital role in protecting fragile mountain ecosystems and preserving the biodiversity of the Everest region.

### Animals Found in Sagarmatha National Park

Sagarmatha National Park is home to several Himalayan mammals and bird species. Some animals are difficult to spot because they live in remote areas or are naturally shy, but the region remains an important destination for nature enthusiasts and wildlife lovers.

![](/images/blog/zydxccrdpjhy89odn4fb.jpg "Sagarmatha National Park")

- **Snow leopard**: One of the most iconic endangered species of the Himalayas, the snow leopard inhabits remote, rugged mountain terrain. Sightings are rare because of its elusive nature and excellent camouflage.
- **Himalayan tahr**: These strong mountain mammals are often found on steep slopes and rocky cliffs. Trekkers may occasionally see them grazing along hillsides, particularly in areas with suitable vegetation.
- **Musk deer**: Found in Himalayan forest and alpine habitats, musk deer are shy animals known for avoiding human activity.
- **Red panda:** Red pandas are associated with temperate forests containing bamboo. Although they are known from parts of the broader eastern Himalayan region, they are not commonly encountered in Sagarmatha National Park, so visitors should not expect to see them.
- **Himalayan marmot**: These ground-dwelling mammals live in high-altitude environments and may be seen around alpine meadows and rocky areas.
- **Pikas**: Small, rabbit-like mammals adapted to cold mountain habitats, pikas can sometimes be spotted among rocks and alpine vegetation.

The chances of seeing wildlife depend on the season, altitude, weather, and trekking route. Visitors should observe animals from a distance and avoid disturbing their natural behaviour.

### Birds of Sagarmatha National Park

Birdlife is another important part of Sagarmatha National Park’s biodiversity. The park’s forests, valleys, and high-altitude landscapes provide habitats for different Himalayan bird species.

Birds that may be found in the region include:

- **Himalayan monal**: Nepal’s national bird, known for its colourful plumage, is associated with Himalayan forest and alpine habitats.
- **Blood pheasant**: A distinctive Himalayan pheasant found in high-altitude environments.
- **Snow pigeon**: Often seen in rocky mountain areas and around cliffs.
- **Golden eagle**: A powerful bird of prey that inhabits open mountain landscapes.
- **Himalayan griffon**: A large vulture that soars above valleys and mountain slopes in search of food.

Birdwatching can be a rewarding activity while trekking through Sagarmatha National Park, especially in forested sections and quieter areas away from busy trails. Binoculars can help visitors observe birds without approaching too closely.

### Plants and Vegetation in Sagarmatha National Park

![](/images/blog/DMI-destination-management-inc-nepal-mountain-nepal-holiday-advisior-national-park-sagarmatha-national-park-scaled-001.jpg "Sagarmatha National Park")

The plants of Sagarmatha National Park vary according to altitude, temperature, soil conditions, and exposure to sunlight. Lower elevations support forests, while higher areas feature shrubs, grasses, mosses, and other vegetation adapted to harsh mountain conditions.

Common vegetation types include:

- **Pine and fir forests**: Found in suitable lower and middle elevations, these forests provide important habitats for birds and mammals.
- **Birch and rhododendron**: These trees and shrubs grow in parts of the Himalayan forest zone, adding seasonal colour to the landscape.
- **Juniper shrubs**: Juniper is well adapted to colder and drier mountain environments.
- **Alpine grasses and wildflowers**: These plants grow in high-altitude meadows during the warmer months, creating colourful landscapes along some trekking routes.
- **Mosses and lichens**: These hardy organisms survive in exposed environments where growing conditions are difficult.

As altitude increases, vegetation becomes more limited due to low temperatures, strong winds, and a shorter growing season. Near the highest mountain areas, plants are sparse, and large sections of the landscape consist of rock, ice, and snow.

## Best Time to Visit Sagarmatha National Park

The best time to visit Sagarmatha National Park is during the **spring (March to May)** and **autumn (late September to November)** seasons. These two periods offer the most stable weather, clearest skies, and best mountain views, making them the preferred trekking seasons for the vast majority of travelers .

### Spring (March to May)

- **Weather and Visibility**: The weather gradually warms up, skies are relatively clear, and it's very suitable for viewing and photographing peaks like Mount Everest.
- **Unique Highlights**: Rhododendrons bloom along lower elevation trails, adding color to the journey .
- **Considerations**: Towards the end of May, precipitation begins to increase, and clouds may start to obscure mountain views.

### Autumn (Late September to November)

- **Weather and Visibility**: After the monsoon ends, skies are exceptionally clear and visibility is excellent, considered the "golden season" for photography and viewing .
- **Temperature**: Days are comfortable, but nights get quite cold, requiring adequate warm clothing.
- **Crowds**: This is also the peak tourist season with more trekkers, so it's advisable to book teahouses and flights in advance.

### Seasons to Avoid

- **Monsoon (June to August)**: Heavy rainfall, slippery trails, and frequent cloud cover obscuring mountain views, plus increased leech activity at lower elevations.
- **Winter (December to February)**: Extreme cold with temperatures well below freezing, some teahouses close, and certain high-elevation passes may be blocked by snow, posing higher risks.

## Sagarmatha National Park Permits and Entry Fees

Trekking in the Sagarmatha National Park (Everest region) requires **two mandatory permits**; the fees for foreign nationals in 2026 total approximately **NPR 6,000 (about 44 USD)**. The specific breakdown is as follows:

### Mandatory Permits and Fees

| Permit Type | Fee (Foreign Nationals) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sagarmatha National Park Entry Permit** | NPR 3,000 | Valid for the entire park (including Gokyo, Three Passes routes), no day limit |
| **Khumbu Pasanglhamu Rural Municipality Permit** | NPR 3,000 | Local government fee, **replaces the old TIMS card**; TIMS is no longer required in the Khumbu region |

> **SAARC country citizens** pay half price for the national park permit (NPR 1,500). Some sources indicate the municipality permit was raised from NPR 2,000 to 3,000 in September 2024 and remains at this price in 2026.

### Where to Purchase

- **Sagarmatha National Park Permit**: Can be purchased at the **Nepal Tourism Board (NTB) office in Kathmandu** (Tourist Service Centre, Bhrikutimandap), or at the **Monjo checkpoint** on the trail. Purchasing in Kathmandu is recommended to avoid queuing at Monjo during peak season.
- **Khumbu Municipality Permit**: Purchased in **Lukla** (after landing) or at the Monjo checkpoint. Note: This permit **cannot be obtained in Kathmandu**, only within the region.

### Inspection Points

Permits are primarily checked at the following locations:

- **Monjo**: Main park entrance, where both permits are inspected
- **Namche Bazaar**: Police checkpoint, where both permits are inspected
- **Phunki Thenga**: National park permit verification

### Practical Tips

- **Bring cash in Nepalese Rupees (NPR)**; card payments are not accepted for permit purchases.
- Permits are valid for the entire EBC route, including extensions to Gokyo Lakes and the Three Passes.
- Children under 10 are typically **exempt from the national park permit fee**, but the municipality permit may still be required; confirm specific details when booking.
