## Autumn (October–November)

![](/images/blog/Chitwan%20National%20Park.jpg%20sadd.jpg "Autumn in Nepal")

Autumn is widely regarded as the **best time to visit Nepal**, and for good reason. The monsoon has just ended, leaving the air washed clean and the Himalayas at their most visible. This is the **best season to visit Nepal** for mountain views, trekking, and sightseeing. October is often called the **best month to visit Nepal**, and if you're asking **is October a good time to visit Nepal**, the answer is a resounding yes.

- **Weather:** Daytime temperatures in Kathmandu range from 20–25°C, while nights cool down to 10–15°C. At higher altitudes, expect much colder nights. Skies are clear, rainfall is minimal, and visibility is at its peak.
- **Why it's the best:** This is the best time to visit Nepal for trekking, including the best time to visit Everest Base Camp. Trails are in excellent condition, teahouses are fully open, and mountain views are unobstructed. It's also a wonderful best time to visit Kathmandu and best time to visit Pokhara, with festivals like Dashain and Tihar bringing color and energy to the streets.
- **What to expect:** Crowds and higher prices. This is peak season, so book flights, permits, guides, and teahouses at least 2–3 months in advance. Trails on Everest, Annapurna, and Langtang get busy.
- **Tips:** Pack layers—days are warm but nights get cold. If you want fewer crowds, aim for late November, but be prepared for colder conditions. Check **Nepal weather by month** to fine-tune your packing and itinerary.

## Spring (March–April)

![](/images/blog/Nagarkot.webp "Spring In Nepal")

Spring is the second **best season to visit Nepal** and a strong contender for the **best time to visit Nepal**. While October may be the **best month to visit Nepal** for many, March and April offer warmer temperatures, blooming rhododendrons, and excellent trekking conditions. It's a great answer to **when to visit Nepal** if you prefer slightly warmer weather.

- **Weather:** Temperatures are pleasant, ranging from 15–28°C in lower elevations. Higher altitudes are warmer than in autumn, making them more comfortable for trekking. However, haze can reduce mountain visibility, and pre-monsoon rain may begin by late May.
- **Why it's great:** This is a fantastic **best time to visit Nepal for trekking**, especially at higher altitudes where autumn can feel colder. It's also the best season for wildlife spotting in Chitwan and Bardia National Parks, as animals come out to drink water. And it remains a good **best time to visit Kathmandu** and **best time to visit Pokhara**.
- **What to expect:** Crowds and higher prices, similar to autumn. Rhododendron forests in the Annapurna and Langtang regions are spectacular.
- **Tips:** Go in early March for clearer views, or late April for warmer temperatures. Book ahead, and check **Nepal weather by month** to avoid the pre-monsoon rains.

## Winter (December–February)

![Winter in Nepal](/images/blog/Trekking-during-winter-in-Nepal.jpg)

Winter isn't the **best time to visit Nepal** overall, nor the **best season to visit Nepal** for most travelers. However, it offers fewer crowds, clear mountain views, and lower prices. It's not the **best time to visit Nepal for trekking** at high altitudes and definitely not the **best time to visit Everest Base Camp**. But if you're asking **when to visit Nepal** for solitude and culture, winter has its charms.

- **Weather:** Cold and dry. Kathmandu temperatures range from 2–18°C, with morning fog common. At altitude, temperatures drop well below freezing. Snow can block high passes above 3,000m.
- **Why it's still worth it:** Lower-altitude treks like Ghorepani Poon Hill and the Kathmandu Valley rim are still doable. It can be a good **best time to visit Kathmandu** and **best time to visit Pokhara** if you don't mind the cold. Chitwan is also pleasant in winter for wildlife.
- **What to expect:** Fewer tourists, lower prices, and quiet trails. Some teahouses and high passes close.
- **Tips:** Pack heavy winter gear. Stick to lower elevations. Check **Nepal weather by month** and trail conditions before you go.

## Monsoon (June–August)

![](/images/blog/view-from-chandragiri-hills.jpg "Monsoon in Nepal")

Monsoon isn't the **best time to visit Nepal**, **best season to visit Nepal**, or **best month to visit Nepal** for most trips. **Nepal weather by month** brings rain, landslides, and leeches. It's not the **best time to visit Nepal for trekking** or the **best time to visit Everest Base Camp**. But for a specific kind of traveler, it's actually the **best time to visit Nepal**.

- **Weather:** Hot, humid, and rainy. Kathmandu gets frequent showers, and trails become muddy and slippery. Mountain views are mostly hidden.
- **Why go anyway:** Rain-shadow regions like Upper Mustang, Dolpo, and Nar-Phu stay dry and are actually at their best. It's also okay for cultural tours in Kathmandu and Pokhara if you don't mind rain. And it's the cheapest time to travel.
- **What to expect:** Fewer tourists, lower prices, and lush green landscapes. Flight delays and road closures are common.
- **Tips:** If you go, choose Mustang or Dolpo. Bring rain gear, expect delays, and be flexible with your itinerary. Check **Nepal weather by month** and regional forecasts carefully.

## Weather Planning

Understanding **Nepal weather by month** is the key to choosing the **best time to visit Nepal**. It helps you decide **when to visit Nepal** and match your trip to the **best season to visit Nepal**.

![](/images/blog/659e3761-526a-4c40-93bf-8c6f0c9ae3fc.png)

**Autumn (Oct–Nov):**

 This is the **best time to visit Nepal** and the **best season to visit Nepal** for most travelers, with October usually the **best month to visit Nepal**. The monsoon clears out the dust, so mountain views are at their sharpest all year. Days are mild (20–25°C in Kathmandu), nights are cold, and the trails are dry and stable—which is why it's the **best time to visit Nepal for trekking** and the **best time to visit Everest Base Camp**. It's also a lively **best time to visit Kathmandu** and **best time to visit Pokhara** thanks to Dashain and Tihar. The catch: peak crowds and prices, so book flights, permits, and teahouses months ahead. **Is October a good time to visit Nepal?** It's the safest bet—though late November gives you the same clear skies with fewer people and colder nights.

**Spring (Mar–Apr):** 

The strong second **best season to visit Nepal**, and for many trekkers a better **best time to visit Nepal for trekking** than autumn, simply because it's warmer up high. Rhododendrons paint the hillsides red and pink from late March through April, especially in Annapurna and Langtang. It's also the best window for wildlife in Chitwan and Bardia, and a good **best time to visit Kathmandu** and **best time to visit Pokhara**. Watch for haze from mid-March and pre-monsoon rain by late May. Crowds and prices match autumn, and Holi adds a fun cultural moment.

**Winter (Dec–Feb):**

 Not the **best time to visit Nepal** overall, and definitely not the **best time to visit Nepal for trekking** at altitude or the **best time to visit Everest Base Camp**. But it works well for culture and lower hikes. Kathmandu runs 2–18°C with morning fog, high passes get snowed in, and many teahouses above Namche close. What's great: empty trails, cheap prices, good wildlife spotting in Chitwan, and lower treks like Poon Hill and Mardi Himal. It can still be a good **best time to visit Kathmandu** and **best time to visit Pokhara** if you pack for the cold.

**Monsoon (Jun–Aug):**

 Rain, landslides, and cancelled Lukla flights make this the wrong season for most trips. It's not the **best time to visit Nepal**, not the **best month to visit Nepal**, and not the **best time to visit Nepal for trekking**. But it's the **best time to visit Nepal** for rain-shadow regions like Upper Mustang, Upper Dolpo, and Nar Phu, which stay dry while the rest of the country soaks. Kathmandu and Pokhara still work for culture—rain usually falls in the afternoon. It's the cheapest season, rivers are prime for rafting, and the countryside is at its greenest. Just build in buffer days.

**Check monthly forecasts:**

 **Nepal weather by month** varies hugely by region and elevation—Mustang in July and Kathmandu in July are different trips. Check forecasts for your specific trek when deciding **when to visit Nepal**.

**Book early:**

 Peak seasons fill fast. For the **best time to visit Nepal**, lock in flights, permits, and teahouses two to three months ahead—sooner for Everest in October.

**Pack layers:**

 Mountain weather changes fast, even in the **best month to visit Nepal**. A warm day at 3,000m can turn freezing by afternoon, so bring a shell and insulation year-round.
