**The best time to visit Nepal is autumn (late September to November) and spring (March to May)**, when skies are clear, trails are dry and the Himalayan views are at their sharpest. Winter suits quieter, lower-altitude trips, and the monsoon (June to August) is best kept for rain-shadow regions such as Upper Mustang.

We are the WaveUp Himalayas team, based in Thamel, Kathmandu, and we have put together this guide so you can pick the right month for your trek, tour or helicopter flight.

***

## When Is the Best Time to Visit Nepal? Nepal Travel Seasons at a Glance

Nepal has four distinct seasons, and the right one depends on what you want to do. Here is the short version.

| Season | Months | Weather | Mountain views | Crowds | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Autumn | Late Sep to Nov | Dry, clear, mild days | Excellent | High | Everest Base Camp, Annapurna Circuit, Langtang, helicopter tours |
| Spring | Mar to May | Warm, flowers in bloom, some haze | Very good (hazier in May) | High | Rhododendron treks, Poon Hill, Everest region |
| Winter | Dec to Feb | Cold, crisp, mostly dry | Clear but cold | Low | Low-altitude treks, Kathmandu, Pokhara, Chitwan |
| Monsoon | Jun to Aug | Heavy rain, humid | Often cloudy | Low | Upper Mustang, Dolpo, green landscapes |

If you want one answer: **October to mid-November** gives the most reliable weather and the clearest mountain views of the year.

## Nepal in October and November: Best Time for Trekking in Nepal (Autumn)

Autumn is the best time to trek in Nepal, and October and November are its strongest months. The monsoon has washed the dust from the air, the rain has stopped and the skies open up to long, clear views of Everest, Annapurna and Manaslu.

![Autumn Trekking in Nepal](/images/blog/Autumn%20trek%20in%20Nepal-001.jpg "Autumn Trekking in Nepal")

What to expect in autumn:

- **Weather:** stable, dry and mild in the hills, with cold nights at altitude. As a rule of thumb, temperatures drop roughly 6°C for every 1,000 m you climb.
- **Views:** the clearest of the year, especially in the early morning.
- **Festivals:** Dashain and Tihar, Nepal's biggest festivals, usually fall between late September and early November. Dates follow the lunar calendar, so check them for your travel year.
- **Crowds:** this is the busiest and most expensive time on the trails, so book early.

Best treks in autumn: the [Everest Base Camp Trek](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/everest-base-camp-trek/), the [Annapurna Circuit Trek](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/annapurna-circuit-trek/), the [Manaslu Circuit Trek](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/manaslu-circuit-trek/) and the Langtang Valley. High passes such as Thorong La (5,416 m) and Larkya La (5,106 m) are usually at their most reliable now.

## Nepal in Spring (March to May): Best Time to See Rhododendrons in Nepal

Spring is the second-best time to visit Nepal and a favourite with photographers and nature lovers. The lower hills fill with blooming rhododendrons, Nepal's national flower, with the best displays usually in late March and April. Days are warm and long, and high routes open up again as the snow retreats.

![Nepal in December to February](/images/blog/Nagarkot.opt-001.jpg "Nepal in December to February")

- **Weather:** warmer than autumn, with afternoon haze building through April and May. Very distant peaks can look softer than in October.
- **Flowers:** April is the standout month for blossoms on trails such as Ghorepani Poon Hill and Annapurna Base Camp.
- **Everest region:** busy in April and May, which is also the main climbing season on Everest.
- **Late May:** heat in the lowlands and the first pre-monsoon showers, so start early each day.

Best treks in spring: the [Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/ghorepani-poon-hill-trek/), the [Annapurna Base Camp Trek](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/annapurna-base-camp-trek/), Everest Base Camp, Langtang and Mardi Himal.

## Is Nepal Worth Visiting in Winter? Nepal in December to February

Winter is Nepal's quietest season. Skies are often brilliantly clear and the trails are peaceful, but it is cold, especially above 3,000 m, where nights fall well below freezing and snow can close high passes. Prices are lower and availability is easier.

![Nepal in December to February](/images/blog/Trekking-during-winter-in-Nepal-001.jpg "Nepal in December to February")

**Winter works well for:**

- Kathmandu Valley and Bhaktapur heritage tours
- Pokhara and lakeside walks
- Chitwan jungle safari, with warm days and cool mornings (fog is possible at dawn)
- Low-altitude treks and Poon Hill, where it is cold and the summit can be snowy
- Helicopter tours and mountain flights on clear mornings

**Think twice about:** Thorong La on the Annapurna Circuit, Larkya La on the Manaslu Circuit and the Everest Three Passes, where snow and ice can make crossings risky or impossible.

Everest Base Camp is possible in winter with proper gear and a flexible schedule, but expect very cold nights and some teahouses at the higher stages to be closed. Tell us your dates and we will say honestly what is realistic.

## Nepal in Monsoon Season: When Is the Rainy Season in Nepal and Where Can You Go?

The monsoon brings most of Nepal's yearly rain. Trails turn muddy, leeches appear in the lower hills, landslides can interrupt roads and cloud often hides the mountains. For that reason we generally do not recommend classic Everest or Annapurna treks in these months, and flights to Lukla can be delayed or cancelled when cloud closes in.

![Nepal in Monsoon Season](/images/blog/view-from-chandragiri-hills-002.jpg "Nepal in Monsoon Season")

The monsoon is not a complete write-off:

- **Upper Mustang and Dolpo** sit in the Himalayan rain shadow and stay comparatively dry.
- **The hills are lush** and the waterfalls are full, which suits photographers who do not mind showers.
- **Cultural tours** in Kathmandu Valley work well with an umbrella and a flexible plan, and crowds and prices are at their lowest.

If you travel now, pack waterproofs, gaiters and leech socks, and keep a few buffer days in your schedule. Rain often lingers into early September.

## Best Time to Visit Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan, and for Helicopter Tours and Festivals

Not every trip to Nepal is a trek. Here is when the rest of our services work best.

- **Kathmandu and Bhaktapur:** October, November, March and April are the most comfortable months for heritage sightseeing. Winter days are pleasant too, with cold mornings and evenings.
- **Pokhara:** September to April is the sweet spot. The monsoon is very wet here, so lake views and paragliding are best outside June to August.
- **Chitwan National Park:** October to March is the most comfortable window for a [jungle safari](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/#jungle-safari), and February to April is often a good time for wildlife sightings. Summers are hot and humid.
- **Lumbini:** October to March is cooler. April to June can be very hot.
- **Helicopter tours:** the best time for an [Everest Base Camp helicopter tour](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/everest-base-camp-heli-tour/) is September to November and March to May, when clear mornings give the best views. Flights depend on weather.
- **Mountain flights:** an [Everest mountain flight](https://waveuphimalayas.com/packages/everest-mountain-flight/) leaves at dawn, and clear autumn and winter mornings are ideal.

**Festivals by month:** Holi (March), Buddha Jayanti (May), Dashain (September to October), Tihar (October to November) and Mani Rimdu in the Everest region (usually October to November). Dates follow the lunar calendar. Dashain and Tihar are wonderful to experience, but transport is busy and many offices close, so book early and allow flexible days.

## Cheapest Time to Visit Nepal: Peak Season vs Off-Season Travel

The cheapest time to visit Nepal is the monsoon (June to August) and deep winter (December to February), when there are fewer visitors and more availability. The trade-off is weather: rain and cloud in summer, cold and snow at altitude in winter.

- **Peak season (October to November, April to May):** the best weather and the busiest trails. Book your trek and any flights as early as you can, ideally a few months ahead.
- **Shoulder weeks (late February to early March, early December):** a good balance of decent weather, fewer people and easier availability.
- **Off-season (June to August, January to February):** lower prices and quiet trails, but you need a flexible schedule and the right gear.

## Best Time to Visit Nepal for First-Time Travelers: Planning Tips

Whichever month you choose, a few habits make every trip safer and more enjoyable.

- **Respect altitude.** Everest Base Camp sits at 5,364 m and Kala Patthar at 5,545 m. Build in acclimatisation days, walk slowly and drink plenty of water. Our guides watch for signs of altitude sickness. If you are unsure how hard a route is, read our guide to [Annapurna Base Camp trek difficulty](https://waveuphimalayas.com/blog/annapurna-base-camp-trek-difficulty/).
- **Pack for the season.** Layers, a warm down jacket and a good sleeping bag matter most from November to March. In the monsoon, waterproofs and leech socks matter most.
- **Add buffer days.** Flights to Lukla can be delayed by weather in any season, so avoid tight international connections right after a trek.
- **Get the right insurance.** Choose cover that includes high-altitude trekking and helicopter evacuation.
- **Match the trip to your time.** Short on days or not keen on long walking? Compare options in our guide to the [Everest Base Camp trek vs helicopter tour](https://waveuphimalayas.com/blog/everest-base-camp-trek-vs-helicopter-tour/).
