overview: |-
  The **Gokyo to Everest Base Camp Trek via Cho La** is a 17-day guided circuit of the Khumbu. After the scenic flight to Lukla, you climb the Gokyo valley to its turquoise lakes, cross the **Cho La pass (5,420 m)**, and finish at **Everest Base Camp (5,364 m)** and **Kala Patthar (5,545 m)** for sunrise on Everest, Nuptse and Pumori.

  Rated as a **challenging high-altitude trek**, the route suits fit trekkers, ideally with some previous multi-day experience. You walk 3 to 9 hours a day, with two **rest days** in Namche Bazaar and Gokyo and a "climb high, sleep low" pattern that puts the highest nights near the end. Cho La is the one technical-feeling day, a steep rocky pass with a short glacier section, and your guide decides on the day whether it is safe to cross.

  Our fully guided package covers all required permits, round-trip Kathmandu–Lukla flights, teahouse lodging, and licensed guide and porter support. It runs from March to May and September to November. **Enquire today for upcoming departure dates with WaveUp Himalayas.**

  - day: 2
    route: Fly to Lukla → Phakding
    altitude: 2,610 m
    duration: 3–4 hrs trek
    description: Fly to Lukla and walk down the Dudh Koshi valley to Phakding.
    image: ''
    highlights: After an early breakfast, transfer to the domestic airport for your flight to Lukla (2,840 m). During peak seasons, flights often operate from Manthali/Ramechhap, which means a pre-dawn drive of 5 to 6 hours from Kathmandu; we confirm the airport before each trip. In Lukla you meet your porters, then follow the river on an easy, mostly downhill trail through villages and past mani walls to Phakding. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 3
    route: Phakding → Namche Bazaar
    altitude: 3,440 m
    duration: 5–6 hrs trek
    description: Cross high suspension bridges, enter Sagarmatha National Park and climb to Namche Bazaar.
    image: ''
    highlights: The trail crosses the Dudh Koshi on several prayer-flag bridges, including the high Hillary Bridge, and enters Sagarmatha National Park at Monjo. The last stretch is a steep climb of about 600 m to Namche, the trading hub of the Khumbu. On a clear day you may glimpse Everest on the way up. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 4
    route: Acclimatization Day in Namche
    altitude: 3,440 m (hike to 3,880 m)
    duration: 3–4 hrs hike
    description: Hike to the Everest View Hotel for a first look at Everest, then return to Namche to sleep.
    image: ''
    highlights: This is an important rest day. Climb to the Everest View Hotel (3,880 m) for views of Everest, Lhotse and Ama Dablam, then come back down to sleep in Namche. Spend the afternoon at the Sherpa museum and the market, drink plenty of water and keep the pace easy. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 5
    route: Namche → Dole
    altitude: 4,040 m
    duration: 6–7 hrs trek
    description: Cross the Mong La ridge, drop to Phortse Thanga and climb to Dole.
    image: ''
    highlights: The trail contours to the Mong La pass, a ridge viewpoint over the Khumbu peaks, then falls through rhododendron and pine forest to the river at Phortse Thanga for lunch. A final climb brings you to Dole. This is a long day, so we start early and walk slowly. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 6
    route: Dole → Machhermo
    altitude: 4,470 m
    duration: 3–4 hrs trek
    description: A short day above the tree line through yak pastures.
    image: ''
    highlights: The path climbs through open scrub and yak pastures above the tree line and reaches Machhermo around midday. In the afternoon an optional short walk uphill, then back down to sleep, helps your body adjust before Gokyo. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 7
    route: Machhermo → Gokyo
    altitude: 4,790 m
    duration: 3–4 hrs trek
    description: Follow the Ngozumpa glacier moraine past the first lakes to Gokyo.
    image: ''
    highlights: The route follows the edge of the Ngozumpa glacier's moraine, passes the first lakes and reaches Gokyo on the shore of its turquoise lake, with Cho Oyu above you. Most of the day is spent in stark, open country. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 8
    route: Gokyo Ri and the Gokyo Lakes
    altitude: 4,790 m (Gokyo Ri 5,357 m)
    duration: 4–5 hrs hike
    description: Sunrise climb of Gokyo Ri, then a free afternoon by the lakes.
    image: ''
    highlights: Leave before dawn for Gokyo Ri, about 2.5 to 3 hours up. From the summit Everest, Lhotse, Makalu and Cho Oyu appear in one sweep, with the glacier and lakes below. The afternoon is free; if you feel strong, walk along the lake toward the fifth lake. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 10
    route: Gokyo → Thangnak
    altitude: 4,700 m
    duration: 3–4 hrs trek
    description: Cross the glacier to Thangnak, the base of the Cho La climb.
    image: ''
    highlights: A short, quiet day across the Ngozumpa glacier on a marked trail to Thangnak (also spelled Dragnag). Lodging here is basic. Your guide checks pass conditions with lodge owners and groups coming the other way before setting the next morning's start. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 10
    route: Thangnak → Cho La Pass → Dzongla
    altitude: About 4,830 m (Cho La 5,420 m)
    duration: 7–9 hrs trek
    description: Cross Cho La before the afternoon wind and descend to Dzongla.
    image: ''
    highlights: The toughest day of the trek. We leave by headlamp at about 4 to 5 am, climb steeply over rock to the pass, and cross a short glacier section on the far side before descending to Dzongla. Microspikes are used where there is snow or ice, and your guide decides whether to use a rope. The views of Ama Dablam and Cholatse from the top are the reward. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 11
    route: Dzongla → Lobuche
    altitude: 4,910 m
    duration: 3–4 hrs trek
    description: A short day that joins the main Everest trail near Lobuche.
    image: ''
    highlights: A short, moderate walk that meets the main trail near Lobuche. Use the afternoon to rest and recover from the pass. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 12
    route: Lobuche → Gorak Shep → Everest Base Camp
    altitude: 5,164 m (Everest Base Camp 5,364 m)
    duration: 7–8 hrs trek
    description: Walk the Khumbu glacier to Gorak Shep, continue to Everest Base Camp and return.
    image: ''
    highlights: Follow the Khumbu glacier's moraine to Gorak Shep, drop your bags, then continue to Everest Base Camp (5,364 m) for a view of the Khumbu Icefall. Everest's summit is hidden behind Nuptse from here. In spring the camp is full of expedition tents; in autumn it is often much quieter. Return to Gorak Shep for the night. Overnight at a teahouse.
  - day: 13
    route: Kala Patthar → Pheriche

    description: Sunrise on Kala Patthar, then a long descent to Pheriche.
    image: ''
    highlights: Start before first light for Kala Patthar (5,545 m), a 1.5 to 2.5 hour climb, for sunrise on Everest, Nuptse and Pumori. It is cold, often -15 °C or below. After a hot breakfast in Gorak Shep, descend nearly 800 m to Pheriche, sleeping much lower. Overnight at a teahouse.
