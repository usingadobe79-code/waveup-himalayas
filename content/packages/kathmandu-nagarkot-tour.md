preparation: |-
  - **Build Basic Fitness:** Walk 30–45 minutes daily for 2–3 weeks before your trip. Focus on uphill walking and stairs to prepare for the Sankhu–Nagarkot hike.
  - **Practice Walking in Hiking Shoes:** Break in your hiking shoes before the trip to avoid blisters on the trail.
  - **Book in Advance:** Reserve your spot early, especially for peak seasons (October–November and March–April).
  - **Arrange Travel Insurance:** Get coverage for medical expenses, trip cancellation, and emergency evacuation.
  - **Check Visa Requirements:** Most nationalities get a Nepal visa on arrival. Carry passport-size photos and USD cash for the fee.
  - **Get Vaccinated:** Consult your doctor about routine vaccines, hepatitis A/B, typhoid, and tetanus. Carry prescription medicines.
  - **Learn Basic Nepali Phrases:** Simple words like _Namaste_ (hello), _Dhanyabad_ (thank you), and _Kati ho?_ (how much?) go a long way.
  - **Research Temple Etiquette:** Know the dress code — cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes before entering temples, and walk clockwise around stupas.
  - **Acclimatize Mentally:** Nagarkot is only 2,195 m, so altitude sickness is not a concern. Stay hydrated and walk at your own pace.
  - **Pack Smart:** Bring layers, hiking shoes, sun protection, a daypack, and a warm jacket for Nagarkot. Leave heavy luggage at your Kathmandu hotel.
  - **Carry Cash:** ATMs are available but can be unreliable. Bring USD and NPR for meals, tips, and small purchases.
  - **Download Offline Maps:** Google Maps may not work well on the trail. Download offline maps of Kathmandu Valley and Nagarkot.
  - **Adjust to Nepal Time:** Nepal is UTC +5:45. Give yourself a day to adjust before the hike.
  - **Prepare for Early Mornings:** The Nagarkot sunrise requires a 5:00 AM wake-up. Sleep early the night before.
  - **Stay Flexible:** Weather, traffic, and local conditions can change plans. Keep a positive attitude and trust your guide.
