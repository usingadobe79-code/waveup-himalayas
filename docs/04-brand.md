# 04 — Brand Guidelines

> **WaveUp Himalayas** — Nepal-based travel company, Thamel, Kathmandu.
> Treks • Tours • Mountain Flights • Helicopter Tours • Jungle Safaris.

## Logo
- Wordmark: **WAVEUP HIMALAYAS** — "WAVE" in navy, "UP" in gold.
- Mark: a navy stylized "W" wave + a gold mountain-peak above the "U".
- Tagline: **Treks • Tours • Flights • Experiences**
- Decorative dashes flank "HIMALAYAS" in gold.

## Typography (suggested — swap if brand has a defined typeface)
- **Display / headings:** a modern geometric or editorial serif/sans with character
  (e.g. "Clash Display", "Sora", or "Fraunces" for an aspirational travel feel).
- **Body:** clean neutral sans (e.g. "Inter", "Geist").
- Headlines: large, confident, generous line-height. Letter-spacing slightly tight on display.

## Voice / tone
- Aspirational, premium, warm. Local expertise without being touristy.
- Hero headline (locked): **"Rise Above the Ordinary"**
- Hero sub (locked): **"Treks, tours & helicopter adventures across the Nepal Himalayas."**

## Motion personality
- Calm, cinematic, premium — NOT flashy or bouncy.
- Eases: prefer custom cubic-bezier ease-out (e.g. `[0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]`).
- Nothing snaps; everything glides.
